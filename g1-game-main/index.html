How to Play

You have 5:00 minutes to unlock all three museum wing seals—Zoology, Arts, and Astronomy—before permanent lockdown.

  • Point & Click (or Tap): Click glowing objects inside each room to inspect clues, collect artifacts, or open combination locks.
  • Cross-Room Artifacts: Every room's Exit Seal requires two unique items discovered in the other two rooms. Switch wings anytime using the top tabs.
  • Using Items: Click an item in your Collected Artifacts bar to equip it, then click the target object or Exit Seal in a room.
  • Keyboard Controls: Press 1, 2, 3 (or ←/→) to switch rooms, Tab + Enter to interact, H for a Hint (-50 pts), M to mute audio, and Esc to close dialogs.
  • Scoring & Penalties: Earn points for items, puzzles, seals, and remaining time. Incorrect lock guesses deduct 25 pts and 15 seconds.
